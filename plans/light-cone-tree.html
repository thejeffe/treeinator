Drawing a design

The tree is a 20-column × 50-row grid: 1,000 pixels. Columns are the ribs; rows are bulb heights.

  • Row pitch is exactly 2" along each rib, set by the strip holes, so every rib's rows line up without measuring.
  • Column pitch shrinks going up: about 6" apart at the ring, 1.4" at the hub. Lay out art on a fan-shaped grid, not a square one, or shapes will pinch toward the top.

Assembly order

  1. Cut and half-lap the two B1 spokes into an X. Glue and screw. Laminate the B2 block, screw it to the X center, and drill the 2" pole socket straight through.
  2. Drill 5/8" stake holes 21" from center on each spoke.
  3. Cut the ring P5 at 115". Mark the 20 rib positions from the left end (table below). Strap the ring to the outside end of each of the four spokes, centered on the 2x4's height (D2 inset).
  4. Make the hub: glue and screw the two B3 discs together. Mark the 20 strip positions around the edge (rib layout table) and put the guy eye under the back at a 6" radius. Bolt the flange underneath.
  5. Cut the pole and EMT. Slide the EMT in flush at the bottom. Dry-fit the flange and disc, check the 102" height, then glue the flange.
  6. Cut 20 strips of 105 holes. Push one 50-pixel string into holes 4, 6, 8 … 102 of each and tuck the extra wire behind. On odd ribs (1, 3, 5 …) the string's input end goes at the bottom (hole 102); on even ribs it goes at the top (hole 4), so data snakes up one strip and down the next.
  7. On site: drive the tent stake at the tree's center, leaving about 4" above grade. Set the base over it with the open side facing the house, drive the four rebar stakes, and slide the pole down over the tent stake.
  8. Hang strips: lay the top tail up the hub edge and over the top at its mark, then drive a #10 screw with a fender washer through it 0.6" in from the edge. Pull the strip down to its ring mark, wrap it over the outside half of the ring, and drive a #8 screw with a washer into the side of the ring. Make it snug, not drum-tight. Alternate left and right sides so the pole stays plumb. Drive the three ring hold-downs midway between the spokes.
  9. Drive the guy stake 60" behind center. Run the cable to the back eye and snug the turnbuckle until the pole is plumb.
  10. Build the topper on the bench: scribe the four rings with a string compass, mark holes from 12 o'clock, push the lights through, route the wiring on the back, and zip-tie the panel to the PVC cross. Push the stub 18" into the EMT and clamp the down arm to it with two hose clamps.
