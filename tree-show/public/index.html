WLED controller
Makes WLED start up blank and go dark (not back to its own designs) when tree-show stops sending. WLED boots off, and its own look is set to plain black; your WLED presets are kept.
Frame rate

Color

At low brightness, rounding each color channel on its own shifts hues. These dim with fractions of a level and round only once. Spatial shares levels between neighboring pixels (by rib and row); temporal flips each pixel between levels over time. In WLED: WLED does gamma and dimming itself at every LED refresh with 16-bit precision, and tree-show sends the plain picture and the brightness. Applies right away.

Tree layout

Text on the tree

Where text sits on the tree and how big it is. The note on the Layout tab shows which pixels the text covers.

Home Assistant (MQTT)

The tree shows up in Home Assistant as a device (MQTT discovery): on/off and brightness, playlist, hold a design, next, schedule, and settings. Needs the MQTT integration (Mosquitto broker) in Home Assistant; log in with a Home Assistant user.
